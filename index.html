Hey, I'm Riley

Mathematician | AI & IT

I'm based in Seattle, WA, currently working remotely at Zoetis — a global animal health company where I apply systems thinking, data analysis, and IT automation to make complex workflows simpler and more reliable.

"I'm passionate about exploring why systems work the way they do and how to make them better."

With a background in Mathematics from the University of Washington Bothell and over a decade of hands-on scripting experience — PowerShell, Power Automate, AWS provisioning — I've found my niche at the intersection of AI strategy and operational IT.

Whether it's automating a reporting pipeline, designing better IT service processes, or applying generative AI concepts to real business challenges, I bring a methodical, first-principles mindset to every problem. I'm a Certified Scrum Product Owner (CSPO) and a continuous learner — always exploring the next frontier in AI, automation, and systems design.

When I'm not engineering workflows or writing about tech, I'm probably digging into what makes complex systems tick — and figuring out how to make them work better.
