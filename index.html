Frequently Asked Questions

What types of companies and team sizes do you typically partner with?
We partner with enterprise technology leaders, fast-growing scale-ups, and established businesses operating complex technical environments. Whether you need senior systems leadership, an emergency database overhaul, or guidance adopting agentic AI pipelines, we bring hands-on depth without the bloat of traditional consulting agencies.
How quickly can an engagement kick off?
Depending on current project commitments, initial discovery sessions and diagnostic audits can typically begin within 1 to 2 weeks of executing mutual NDAs. For critical production incidents or emergency architecture reviews, expedited onboarding is available.
How are engagements structured and billed?
We prioritize transparent, predictable billing. Scoped projects are structured with clear milestone-based fixed fees so you never face unexpected billing surprises. Fractional engineering partnerships are structured via monthly retainers with guaranteed weekly availability. Detailed proposals and transparent estimates are provided following the initial discovery call.
Are engagements conducted remotely or on-site?
Engagements are conducted remotely from Seattle, Washington (Pacific Time), allowing seamless collaboration with clients across North America and globally. For Seattle-area organizations or critical architectural kickoff workshops, on-site working sessions can be arranged.
How do you protect confidentiality, data privacy, and intellectual property?
We execute standard bilateral non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) prior to reviewing any proprietary systems or codebase access. 100% of custom code, scripts, documentation, and architecture deliverables created during an engagement belong exclusively to you, with zero proprietary lock-in.
Can a project sprint transition into an ongoing advisory retainer?
Yes, many clients start with a focused 2–4 week project sprint (e.g. database performance tuning or automation framework build) and transition into a fractional advisory retainer to maintain architectural continuity and support their engineering leads on an ongoing basis.
